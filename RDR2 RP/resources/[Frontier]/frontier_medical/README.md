# Frontier Medical V1 (ACE-inspired, 1899)
## Install
Back up DB. Apply frontier_needs/sql/migrations/003_labour.sql and frontier_medical/sql/migrations/001_medical.sql (after prior migrations). Deploy all changed resources. Start frontier_needs before frontier_medical; keep vorp_medic and vorp_metabolism stopped. Actual host server.cfg not tracked. Full restart with players disconnected recommended. New characters and existing characters start with no medical injuries once, then persist. Keep VORP Core character/death/respawn lifecycle for now.

## Gameplay
/medical opens self examination; /medical <server ID> opens a patient within 3m in the same routing bucket. Select a body region, examine it, check pulse, and use first-aid supplies from the examining player's satchel. Treatments take five seconds and are revalidated at completion. Bandage controls bleeding, splint stabilises a fracture, tonic temporarily reduces perceived pain (not native health). Medicine wheel or inventory self-use chooses the most severe eligible wound and is immediate. Existing bandage and tonic plus new splint item ID. Basic first aid is available to all players; doctor-only surgery and licences are future work. Splints must be supplied through existing admin/item provisioning until recipes/shops are configured.

Medical state is durable: seven regions, aggregate wound severity, bleeding, fracture, splint, pain, blood volume, temporary analgesia, downed state, life counter. Pulse is qualitative and examination only reveals selected body regions; no omniscient display. Blood loss ticks every five seconds. Recovery requires controlled bleeding and nonempty nutrition reserves (>10%). Fractures must be stabilised to heal. At low blood or very high pain players become unconscious, with client ragdoll/control restrictions; wake after sufficient recovery. Blood depletion requests fatal native damage. Native fatal hits still follow VORP death/respawn; V1 does NOT intercept lethal engine hits or guarantee revival. Observed native death followed by respawn resets medical state; fast death/respawn between ticks may be missed. Injury detection uses the player's own bounded health-loss reports, inferred damage type and last bone; RedM native hit regions/type need live validation and unknown bones fall back to chest. Starvation damage from Needs is excluded from trauma best-effort. No native health restoration by bandages or tonic.

Treatments prepare a durable outbox and apply only after matching completed inventory receipt, using atomic state CAS plus applied marker. Menu consumes from treating player's inventory; self wheel uses existing inventory commit. If patient dies or wound changes before effect applies, item may be spent without effect. No duplication on replay. No offline bleeding progression; tonic uses wall-clock expiry. Client damage/control reports are not anti-cheat. Medical does not replace native combat damage or provide a complete server-authoritative incapacitation gate for every gameplay system; labour and client physical controls are integrated. Later: robust native injury adapter, airway/oxygenation/shock, surgery, infection, medical businesses, doctor training, patient records, rescue from native death.
