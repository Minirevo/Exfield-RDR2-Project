# Frontier Business — foundation preview, not production-ready
Apply sql/001_business.sql with the resource stopped, after existing Frontier migrations. Restart Economy and Inventory, then ensure frontier_business after Property. /business opens the management UI; registry purchases require proximity to a configured office. Office positions and fees are provisional. This branch implements initial registration, shares, ranks, invitations, payroll, financial proposals, tax accrual, licenses, and property authorization hooks. **Do not deploy to a live economy.** Remaining work: complete physical company storage and retail, Trade integration, receipt issuance, rank editing/accepted wage changes, property grant UI, license surrender/debt settlement, atomic multi-recipient dividends and ownership proposal recovery, reliable online time accounting, liquidation and final archival, access/privacy review, automated and live tests. Liquidating companies currently stop operations but are deliberately not destroyed. Existing resources remain independent. Business account provisioning currently inserts a zero-balance account into Economy; monetary movements use Economy's existing idempotent ledger API. No existing data is dropped.
